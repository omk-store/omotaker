Omo Taker is more than one song. This is a journey from Benin City to Lagos, built through pressure, belief, movement and a refusal to stop.

From Benin City with a dream bigger than where I started.

My name is Omo Taker. I am an independent Nigerian artist, dancer and creator. Music has always been my way of turning real experiences into something people can feel.

I moved with a vision, carrying my sound from Benin to Lagos. There was no perfect team waiting for me and no easy road. I learned, invested, created content, promoted myself and continued building even when the results did not come immediately.

Every release represents a different part of my life. Some songs carry pain, some carry energy, some carry love, and some are made for people to dance and forget their problems.

OMK means Only My Kind. It is not just a name. It represents originality, confidence and becoming the person you were created to be.

This page is not about one moment or one record. It is the full world of Omo Taker—and the journey is still being written.
