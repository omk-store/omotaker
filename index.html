The Career Story

Omo Taker started as an independent artist with a strong belief in his sound. His journey moved from early records like Badt Guy to stronger releases like Bend Down Select, Benin To Lagos, If Earth Could Talk, and Follow Follow.

My Truth EP represents the foundation: the first full body of work that shows the artist, the brand, the lifestyle, and the wolf energy behind OMK.

About Omo Taker

Omo Taker is an Afrobeats artist from Benin, Edo State, Nigeria. His sound carries street rhythm, personal truth, and the confidence of an independent artist building from the ground up.

Powered by OMK, his movement connects music, fashion, lifestyle, and visual storytelling. This is not just promotion — this is the beginning of the wolf community.
