THE JOURNEY

My Story Before The Sound

I came into music with nothing but a dream, hunger, and belief. From Benin City to Lagos, I have been building my name step by step.

My first year, I pushed my way into the Apple Music Nigeria Top 200 with Badt Guy. That moment showed me that the dream was possible.

My second year, Bend Down Select started moving and crossed 50,000+ streams. It proved that my sound could travel beyond where I started.

Today, I am still learning, still recording, still building, and still turning real life into music. Follow Follow is the next chapter of that journey.

So before you press play, understand this: you are not just listening to a song. You are joining the movement of an artist who refused to stop.
