THE REAL STORY

From A Dream To A Movement

A few years ago, I entered the music industry with nothing but a dream.

No major label. No big machine. Just belief, hunger, and the courage to keep going even when the journey was not easy.

I come from Benin City, Nigeria. Every step I took into music came with pressure, lessons, and the need to prove that my sound could travel beyond where I started.

My first year, I made it into the Apple Music Nigeria Top 200 with Badt Guy. That moment showed me that people were listening.

My second year, Bend Down Select broke through and found its audience on Spotify. More people discovered the sound, and the movement kept growing.

Then came Benin To Lagos, a record that carried my journey, my roots, and my ambition from one city to another.

Today, I am still learning, still growing, and still building independently. I can record myself, build my sound, and keep improving every day.

FOLLOW FOLLOW is the newest chapter. It was created from real experience, street energy, and the truth that everybody has followed the wrong person, wrong path, or wrong direction before.

But the story does not end there. You learn. You grow. You keep moving forward.

This is more than a song. This is the journey of Omo Taker. Welcome to the OMK movement. 🐺
