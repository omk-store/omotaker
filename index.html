ABOUT THE ARTIST

From Benin with a bigger dream.

Music built from real experiences.

My name is Omo Taker. I am an independent Nigerian artist, dancer and creator. Music has always been my way of turning real experiences into something people can feel.

I moved with a vision, carrying my sound from Benin City to Lagos. There was no perfect team waiting for me and no easy road. I learned, invested, created, promoted myself and continued building.

Every release represents a different part of my life. Some songs carry pain, some carry love, while others are made for people to dance and forget their problems.

OMK means Only My Kind. It represents originality, confidence and becoming the person you were created to be.

This is not about one moment or one record. It is the full world of Omo Taker, and the journey is still being written.
