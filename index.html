About Omo Taker

Omo Taker is an independent Nigerian Afrobeats artist from Benin City. His sound blends street confidence, emotional truth, melody, and lifestyle energy.

Powered by OMK, his movement connects music, fashion, visuals, storytelling, and a growing community built around originality, pressure, and wolf energy.
