THE REAL STORY

Built By Pressure

Before the followers, before the streams, before the movement, there was just a dream and the courage to keep going.

A few years ago, I entered music with nothing but a dream.

No major label. No big machine. Just belief, hunger, and pressure.

I come from Benin City, Nigeria. Every step into music came with lessons, sacrifice, and the need to prove that my sound could travel beyond where I started.

Badt Guy reached Apple Music Nigeria Top 200. Bend Down Select found its audience. Benin To Lagos carried my journey. Now Follow Follow is the next chapter.
